\chapter{The texts}

\begin{objetivo}
Build the study list: the texts that treat the theme, grouped by subject and
with the weight of each one.
\end{objetivo}

The list has \temaNTextos\ texts in \temaNGrupos\ groups. It is a selection:
the appendix shows the \temaNOcorrencias\ occurrences of the words of the
theme, with those already on the list highlighted.
